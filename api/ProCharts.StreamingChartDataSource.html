Remarks

Appending is O(1) per sample and does not shift retained history. AppendRange raises one invalidation, outside the lock, on the calling thread. UI hosts must marshal notifications to their UI thread. Returned snapshots own their data and remain stable after later appends. Configure a numeric or date category axis when samples do not provide category labels.
