Remarks

Styles and formatter delegates keep their normal shared-reference semantics; numerical storage is owned by the source. In the current Skia renderer, Line/Area are category-indexed and Scatter supports numeric X positioning. Storing shared X values does not change that renderer contract. Map category hits through the captured view's original-row identities.
